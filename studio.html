A studio for stories that belong to the people who live them

Livin Up is a creative studio for brands rooted in art, culture and people. We shoot, film and design alongside the communities we serve.

A studio built around people

We ask before we photograph, credit before we publish, and work with local makers, printers and crews wherever possible. That means the story feels honest, useful and owned by the community it represents.

How we work

  • We ask first. Permission comes before every portrait and every film.
  • We credit and pay. The people in front of the lens are named and paid for their time.
  • We work locally. Local crews, printers and makers wherever we can.
  • We hand it all over. You keep the files, the licence and the story.
